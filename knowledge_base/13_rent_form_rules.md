# Rent Form and Extraction Rules (Ind AS 116)

category: EXTRACTION_RULES
topic: Lease Extraction Guidelines
subtopic: Regulatory Audit
priority: 1
keywords: rent, period, gst, working period, lock-in, incentive, direct cost, purchase option

## Assessment Scope (Qualitative & Quantitative)
1. Your assessment should be qualitative as well as quantitative which is required for measuring rent and other quantitative measures such as in-substance fixed lease payments.

## Period of Lease and Working Period
1. **Total Lease Period:** The total agreement period to be considered in "Period of lease" must include both the non-cancellable period and the cancellable period.
2. **Lease Working Period Determination:**
   - Include the non-cancellable period of the lease.
   - Include periods covered by an extension option if the lessee is reasonably certain to exercise it.
   - Include periods covered by a termination option if the lessee is reasonably certain NOT to exercise it.
   - **Enforceability Limit:** A lease is no longer enforceable when both the lessee and the lessor each have the unilateral right to terminate the lease without permission from the other party and with no more than an insignificant penalty.
   - **Lessee Unilateral Termination Right:** If only the lessee has the right to terminate the lease, it is treated as an option to terminate the lease. Under this condition, the tool must ask management: *"Within what period does the lessee expect to terminate the lease?"*
   - **Lessor Unilateral Termination Right:** If only the lessor has the right to terminate, the non-cancellable period of the lease includes the entire period covered by the option to terminate.
3. **Lock-In Period:**
   - The lock-in period must be set to the exact same duration as the calculated **Lease Working Period**.

## Payment Details and Frequency
1. **Rent Payment Type:**
   - Rent payment details must default to **"Advance Payment"** unless **"Arrear Payment"** is explicitly mentioned in the rent agreement.
2. **Interest Calculation Frequency:**
   - Must always be set to **"Monthly"**.
3. **Rent Payment Frequency:**
   - Use the frequency specified in the agreement. If nothing is specified, default to **"Monthly"**.

## Rent Amount Components & Clarifications
1. **Rent Amount Calculations:**
   - Include the base rent amount from the agreement. Deduct any lease incentives from the Right-of-Use (ROU) Asset.
   - **Variable Index/Rate Payments:** Variable lease payments depending on an index or rate must be measured using the value at the commencement date. If the value of that index or rate at the commencement date is NOT specified in the agreement, ask management: *"What is the value of the index or rate at the commencement date?"*
   - **Residual Value Guarantees:** If residual value guarantees are specified in the agreement, ask management: *"What is the expected residual value guarantee amount at the commencement date?"*
   - **Purchase Options:** If a purchase option is mentioned in the agreement but certainty is not specified, ask management: *"Is the lessee reasonably certain to exercise the purchase option?"*. If the answer is "Yes" and the purchase price is missing from the contract, ask management: *"What is the purchase option price?"*
   - **Termination Penalties:** Include penalties for terminating the lease if the lease term reflects the lessee exercising an option to terminate.
   - **In-substance Fixed Payments:** These are payments that contain variability in form but are unavoidable in substance. In case there is variable lease payments mentioned in the agreement, AI must assess whether there is any in-substance fixed lease rent mentioned in the agreement. If in-substance fixed payments are mentioned but the amount is not clear, ask management: *"What is the amount of the in-substance fixed lease payments?"*

## GST Applicability
1. **GST Calculation Rule:**
   - Ask management **every time** an agreement is processed: *"Should GST be included in the rent amount?"*
   - If management confirms GST should be included, ask: *"What is the GST rate?"*
   - If rent is ₹1,00,000 and GST is 18%, the tool must compute the total rent amount as ₹1,18,000.

## ROU Adjustments (Incentives and Direct Costs)
1. **Lease Incentives (Negative Balance):**
   - Deduct lease incentives received or receivable. 
   - If a lease incentive is mentioned but the date is not, ask management: *"What is the date of the lease incentive?"*
   - If a lease incentive is receivable on a certain date, ask management: *"Is this lease incentive expected to be received?"*
   - **Note:** Do NOT ask about lease incentives if the agreement makes no mention of them.
2. **Initial Direct Costs (Positive Balance):**
   - Add initial direct costs incurred by the lessee.
   - If nothing is mentioned in the agreement, ask management **every time** an agreement is processed: *"Were there any initial direct costs incurred by the lessee at the initiation of the agreement?"*
